# SVATEH Admin Guide

Your store is managed entirely from **svateh.com/admin**: no code, no developer needed. Every change you save goes live immediately.

## Signing in
- Go to **svateh.com/admin** and sign in with your email and password.
- Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen and we’ll email you a reset link.
- Change your name or password any time: click your name at the bottom of the menu.

## The dashboard
Shows today’s and the last 30 days’ revenue, orders waiting to be packed, pending reviews and messages, low-stock shades, and a **Launch checklist** of anything still to set up.

## Products
**Products → New product**
1. **Basics:** name, product type (e.g. “Mousse lipstick”), short descriptor, category and status. Keep it on **Draft** until it’s ready.
2. **Pricing:** price in ₹. Use *Compare-at price* only for genuine offers (shown struck through).
3. **Images:** drag photos in. The first is the main image; use the arrows to reorder.
4. **Shades & stock:** one row per shade: name, a unique SKU, swatch colour, stock, and optionally skin depth/undertone (used for shade guidance). A product with no shades needs one variant called “Standard”.
5. **Product details:** What it is, Why you’ll love it, texture/finish/coverage, How to use (steps), full Ingredients list, shade guidance, product FAQs and *Complete your look* products.
6. Set **Status → Published** and **Save**.

The store won’t publish a product without *How to use* and *Ingredients*. Use **Duplicate** to start a similar product quickly (copies are saved as drafts).

Switch on **Show in The SVATEH Edit** to feature a product on the homepage. Only mark **Bestseller** with real sales evidence. Until then the menu shows “New Launches” (change the label in Site settings).

## Categories
Face, Lips, Cheeks, Eyes… A category with no published products is hidden from the menu automatically.

## Homepage
**Homepage** lists every block in display order: hero, gold strip, The Edit, Beauty That Does More, Shop Your Mood, 5-Minute Face, Why SVATEH, Shade Finder, SVATEH Girls, Reviews, Our Story, Newsletter.
- Open a block to change its text, images, buttons and background colour.
- Change **Display order** to move it; switch off **Visible** to hide it.
- **Duplicate** a block to add another (e.g. a second image banner).

The announcement bar, logo, brand colours, contact details and social links are in **Site settings**.

## Moods, Routines, Bundles, Build Your Own Bag, Shade Finder
- **Moods** (Pink Mood, Nude Mood, Soft Glam, Date Night, Glow Mode): description, colour, finished-look image, tutorial video link, products, application steps and a complete-look bundle.
- **Routines:** steps in order with a product for each. Customers choose shades and add the whole routine.
- **Bundles:** pick products and set the bundle price. The saving is shown automatically. Check margins before discounting.
- **Build Your Own Bag:** choose which products are allowed for each step and an optional saving, then switch it on.
- **Shade Finder:** add a row for each skin depth + undertone pointing to a real shade (the shade name must match the product exactly). Optional “existing foundation” matches power the brand step.

## Orders
1. New orders arrive in **Orders** as *Placed*, and you get an email.
2. Open the order → **Invoice & packing slip** to print.
3. Change status to **Packed**, then **Shipped**, adding the courier, tracking number and tracking link. The customer is emailed automatically (untick the box if you don’t want that).
4. Mark **Delivered** when it arrives. COD orders are marked paid at the same time.
5. **Cancelled** returns stock to sale. For online payments, issue the refund in your Razorpay dashboard, then set **Refunded**.

Online orders that were never paid (the customer closed the payment window) are cancelled automatically and their stock released. Export orders to CSV from the Orders page.

## Shipping & payments
Set the free-shipping threshold, delivery fees and times, express delivery, which PIN codes you deliver to, Cash on Delivery on/off with fee and minimum/maximum order value, and PIN codes without COD. Customers see these rules instantly in their bag and at checkout.

## Coupons
Percentage off, flat ₹ off or free shipping, with optional minimum order, maximum discount, total uses and start/end dates. Customers enter the code in their bag or at checkout.

## Reviews & SVATEH Girls
- Customer reviews arrive as **Pending**. Approve genuine reviews to publish them; you can add a public reply. “Verified purchase” is added automatically when the reviewer’s email matches a delivered order. **Never add invented reviews.**
- **SVATEH Girls:** add creator/customer posts with an image, link and the products used. Tick **I have permission to use this content** before publishing.

## Pages & FAQs
- **Pages** contains About, Shipping Policy, Returns & Refunds, Privacy Policy and Terms. Each page is at svateh.com/<url-slug>. You can add new pages too.
- Replace every **[PLACEHOLDER]** with your final, legally reviewed wording before launch.
- **FAQs** are grouped by category on the FAQ page.

## Messages & subscribers
Contact-form messages appear in **Messages** (and your inbox). Mark them *Replied* or *Closed*. **Subscribers** lists newsletter sign-ups. Export to CSV for your email tool.

## Site settings
Brand name, logo, favicon, brand colours, announcement bar, menu options, contact details (only verified ones), social links, footer text, default SEO title/description, share image, Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel IDs (they load only after visitors accept cookies).

## Team members
Owners can add people under **Admin users**:
- **Owner:** everything, including team members
- **Manager:** everything except team members
- **Editor:** products, collections, homepage and pages only (no orders, customers or settings)

## Need a developer?
Only for changes to the design itself or new features. Everything listed above you can do yourself.
